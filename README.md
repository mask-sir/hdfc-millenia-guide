🚀 Step 1: Upgrade to Millennia Debit Card

If you already have an HDFC Bank account but don't have a Millennia Debit Card:

1. Open the HDFC Bank app.
2. Look for the debit card upgrade option.
3. Select Millennia Debit Card if available.
4. Follow the instructions to complete your request.

Fees: ₹499 + GST for joining and ₹499 + GST annually, subject to the bank's current fee schedule.

---

📲 Step 2: Set Up PayZapp Wallet

1. Download "HDFC PayZapp" (https://www.hdfcbank.com/personal/payments/payzapp) from the Play Store.
2. Register or log in.
3. Complete the required wallet KYC.
4. Make sure your wallet is ready for eligible transactions.

---

💰 Step 3: Earn Up to ₹400 Monthly

Use your HDFC Millennia Debit Card for eligible wallet reloads.

- Cashback rate: 1%
- Monthly maximum: ₹400 in reward points
- Eligible spending target: ₹40,000 per month

🧮 Example Transactions

You don't need to add ₹40,000 in a single transaction.

Transaction| Amount
1| ₹1,000
2| ₹5,000
3| ₹15,000
4| ₹19,000
Total| ₹40,000
Potential reward points at 1%| ₹400 equivalent

The transactions must qualify for the card's cashback program and fall within its applicable monthly limit.

---

✈️ Step 4: Domestic Airport Lounge Access

By meeting the applicable quarterly spending requirement, you may qualify for complimentary domestic airport lounge access.

- Check the current quarterly spending requirement.
- Confirm your eligibility before travelling.
- Follow any voucher generation or validation process required by HDFC Bank.

Important: Lounge access is subject to the bank's current terms and conditions. Spending ₹40,000 alone should not be treated as automatic confirmation of lounge eligibility.

---

🎁 Step 5: Redeem Your Reward Points

1. Open the HDFC Bank app or the applicable rewards portal.
2. Navigate to the reward points redemption section.
3. Check your available points.
4. Redeem according to the bank's applicable redemption rules and minimum requirements.

Reward points are not necessarily the same as cash credited directly to your bank account.

---

🔄 Step 6: Reuse Your Wallet Balance

After loading your wallet, check whether PayZapp permits transferring the balance to your own bank account.

If permitted, you can reuse the balance for further eligible transactions, subject to wallet limits, fees, and applicable terms.

Always verify transaction eligibility and charges before repeating wallet loads.
