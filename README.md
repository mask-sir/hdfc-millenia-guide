💳 HDFC Millennia Debit Card – Setup Guide

Learn how to set up your HDFC Millennia Debit Card and maximize your eligible cashback rewards.

1. Upgrade to Millennia Debit Card

If you have an HDFC Bank account but don't have a Millennia Debit Card, check the HDFC Bank app for the option to upgrade your existing debit card.

- Joining fee: ₹499 + GST
- Annual fee: ₹499 + GST

2. Set Up PayZapp Wallet

1. Download and install "PayZapp" (https://www.hdfcbank.com/personal/payments/payzapp).
2. Complete the required KYC process for your PayZapp Wallet.

3. Earn Up to ₹400 Cashback Points per Month

1. Add a total of ₹40,000 to your PayZapp Wallet during a month using your HDFC Millennia Debit Card.
2. You can split the amount into multiple transactions instead of adding it all at once.

Example:

- ₹1,000
- ₹5,000
- ₹15,000
- ₹19,000

Total: ₹40,000

Eligible wallet reloads earn 1% cashback points, up to the applicable monthly limit of ₹400, subject to the bank's terms and conditions.

4. ✈️ Domestic Airport Lounge Access

Completing the required spending may also qualify you for complimentary domestic airport lounge access.

Check the applicable quarterly spending requirement, lounge eligibility, and access conditions with HDFC Bank before travelling.

5. Redeem Your Reward Points

- Cashback is credited as reward points.
- Redeem your points through HDFC Bank's available redemption options.
- Redemption is subject to the applicable rules and minimum redemption requirements.

6. Repeat the Process

After loading your wallet, you may transfer the balance to your own bank account if PayZapp permits the transaction. Check the wallet's applicable terms, fees, and transaction limits before proceeding.
