🚀 Step 1: Upgrade to Millennia Debit Card

If you already have an HDFC Bank account but don't have a Millennia Debit Card:

1. Open the HDFC Bank app.
2. Look for the debit card upgrade option.
3. Select Millennia Debit Card, if available.
4. Follow the instructions to complete your request.

Joining Fee: ₹499 + GST
Annual Fee: ₹499 + GST

Fees and upgrade availability are subject to HDFC Bank's current terms.

---

📲 Step 2: Set Up PayZapp Wallet

1. Download and install "HDFC PayZapp" (https://www.hdfcbank.com/personal/payments/payzapp) from the Play Store.
2. Register or log in to your account.
3. Complete the required KYC process for your wallet.
4. Ensure your wallet is ready for eligible transactions.

---

💰 Step 3: Earn Up to ₹400 Cashback Points Per Month

Use your HDFC Millennia Debit Card for eligible PayZapp Wallet reloads.

- Cashback rate: 1%
- Monthly maximum: ₹400 in reward points
- Monthly spending target: ₹40,000 in eligible transactions

🧮 Example Transactions

You don't need to add the entire ₹40,000 in one transaction. You can split it into multiple transactions.

Transaction| Amount
1| ₹1,000
2| ₹5,000
3| ₹15,000
4| ₹19,000
Total| ₹40,000
Potential Cashback| ₹400

Note: Cashback is subject to eligible transaction categories, monthly caps, and HDFC Bank's applicable terms.

---

✈️ Step 4: Domestic Airport Lounge Access

By meeting the applicable quarterly spending requirement, you may qualify for complimentary domestic airport lounge access.

- Check the current quarterly spending requirement.
- Confirm your eligibility before travelling.
- Follow any voucher generation or validation process required by HDFC Bank.

«Important: Lounge access is subject to the bank's current terms and conditions. Meeting the ₹40,000 monthly spending target does not automatically guarantee lounge access unless the applicable quarterly eligibility requirements are also met.»

---

🎁 Step 5: Redeem Your Reward Points

1. Open the HDFC Bank app or the applicable rewards portal.
2. Navigate to the reward points redemption section.
3. Check your available reward points.
4. Redeem according to the bank's applicable rules and minimum redemption requirements.

Remember: Reward points are not necessarily equivalent to cash credited directly to your bank account.

---

🔄 Step 6: Reuse Your Wallet Balance

After loading your wallet:

1. Check whether PayZapp permits transferring the balance to your own bank account.
2. Verify any applicable fees, transaction limits, and restrictions.
3. Reuse the balance only if permitted by the wallet's terms and conditions.

Always verify transaction eligibility and charges before repeating wallet reloads.
