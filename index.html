🚀 Step 1: Upgrade Your Debit Card

01

Get the Millennia Debit Card

For existing HDFC Bank account holders

  1. Open your HDFC Bank app.
  2. Look for the debit card upgrade option.
  3. Select Millennia Debit Card, if available.
  4. Follow the instructions to complete the request.
Joining fee ₹499 + GST
Annual fee ₹499 + GST

Verify the latest fees and upgrade availability with HDFC Bank.

📲 Step 2: Set Up PayZapp

02

Prepare Your Wallet

Complete the setup before making any reloads.

  1. Download PayZapp from the Play Store.
  2. Register or log in to your account.
  3. Complete the required wallet KYC.
  4. Check your wallet's loading and transfer limits.
Visit PayZapp ↗

💰 Step 3: Target ₹400 Monthly Rewards

03

Use Eligible Wallet Reloads

Based on the 1% reward rate described in this guide.

  • Target total eligible transactions of ₹40,000 per month.
  • You can split the amount across multiple transactions.
  • Eligible transactions may earn reward points up to the monthly cap.

✈️ Step 4: Airport Lounge Access

04

Check Your Quarterly Eligibility

Complimentary domestic lounge access, subject to conditions.

  • Check the current quarterly spending requirement.
  • Confirm that your debit card qualifies for the benefit.
  • Check whether a lounge voucher is required.
  • Verify the participating airports and lounges before travelling.
⚠️ Important
The ₹40,000 monthly spending target for cashback does not, by itself, confirm lounge eligibility. Lounge benefits have separate terms and qualifying conditions. Check the latest HDFC Bank rules before travel.

🎁 Step 5: Redeem Reward Points

05

Check Your Rewards

Use the applicable HDFC Bank redemption option.

  1. Open the HDFC Bank app or applicable rewards portal.
  2. Find the reward points redemption section.
  3. Check your available points and redemption options.
  4. Redeem according to the current rules.

Reward points may have minimum redemption requirements and should not automatically be treated as cash credited to your bank account.

🔄 Step 6: Reuse Your Wallet Balance

  1. Check whether PayZapp permits transferring your wallet balance to your own bank account.
  2. Review any applicable fees, limits, and restrictions.
  3. Reuse the balance only when the wallet's terms permit it.
  4. Check transaction eligibility before repeating wallet reloads.
Never assume that every reload or transfer qualifies for rewards. Confirm the applicable wallet and card terms first.
